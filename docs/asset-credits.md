# Asset and licensing record

- The active player scene uses **Cartoon Asian Boy** by **giyas3dartist**, downloaded from [Fab](https://www.fab.com/listings/27f01754-2e68-44f1-a819-b8968d0cac01). The project owner confirmed this exact source and its Fab Standard License. The active `models/AsianBoy_animated.glb` was produced with the scripts under `tools/`; its Blender source and generated intermediate are retained under ignored `.local/archive/player-authoring/` and `.local/archive/generated-player/`. The player scene scales the active model to 0.75. Fab permits the asset to be incorporated into a distributed game, but not redistributed on its own.
- Idle and Walking motions came from Adobe Mixamo downloads, retained under ignored `.local/archive/player-authoring/mixamo/` and retargeted onto the Fab model's skeleton. [Adobe's Mixamo FAQ](https://helpx.adobe.com/creative-cloud/faq/mixamo-faq.html) says its animations may be used royalty free in personal, commercial, and nonprofit video games. The FBXs are authoring inputs; the active game includes the baked motions inside `AsianBoy_animated.glb`.
- Pip uses [Free Cute Animated Yellow Chick](https://www.fab.com/listings/b888a41b-fafe-4775-86a5-cbc9c5f9b7ad) by **Lucy character store**. The project owner supplied the source archive, kept under ignored `.local/archive/bird-authoring/`. `tools/build_yellow_chick.py` uses its Idle, Fly, and Feed FBXs plus base-color atlas to build the active `vocab-box/models/YellowChick_animated.glb`. The artist's `Read Before Use/Text.txt` permits editing and use in games but asks that the source model not be shared or resold separately. These source files are authoring inputs, not game dependencies.
- The city shop exteriors use **City Kit Commercial 2.1** by **Kenney**, supplied by the project owner in `vocab-box/models/kenney_city-kit-commercial_2.1/`. The included `License.txt` identifies the pack as CC0 and permits personal, educational, and commercial use. Its [official asset page](https://kenney.nl/assets/city-kit-commercial) also identifies it as CC0. `scenes/city_shops.tscn` instances `building-e.glb`, `building-j.glb`, and `building-k.glb` for Sunny Market, the library, and the bakery. Collision, signs, and interactions are authored in that scene. `scenes/city_backdrop.tscn` also instances the pack's `building-a`, `-c`, `-d`, `-f`, `-g`, `-i`, and low-detail `-a`/`-h` GLBs for non-interactive background blocks. `scenes/city_outer_blocks.tscn` reuses low-detail `-a`/`-h` for its far skyline.
- The outdoor block uses **City Kit Roads 2.2** and **City Kit Suburban 2.0** by **Kenney**, supplied by the project owner in `vocab-box/models/kenney_city-kit-roads/` and `vocab-box/models/kenney_city-kit-suburban_20/`. Both included `License.txt` files state CC0. `scenes/city_ground.tscn` instances the packs' pavement and road tiles, crossing, streetlights, trees, and two houses. Invisible collision shapes bound the walkable block. `scenes/city_backdrop.tscn` reuses road and pavement GLBs plus suburban `building-type-b`, `-d`, `-n`, and `tree-small` for the decorative blocks. `scenes/city_outer_blocks.tscn` adds Road Kit `road-crossroad.glb` and reuses its straight road and pavement, plus Suburban `building-type-b`, `-d`, `-f`, `-n`, `-s`, `-u`, `tree-large`, and `tree-small` GLBs. Sources: [Roads](https://kenney.nl/assets/city-kit-roads), [Suburban](https://kenney.nl/assets/city-kit-suburban).
- The pavement albedo in `vocab-box/models/textures/pavement.png` is **Stone Brick Pavement 003** by **Katsukagi** from [3DTextures.me](https://3dtextures.me/2016/09/06/stone-brick-pavement-003/), supplied by the project owner. The creator describes it as a seamless 1024×1024 texture; the site's [license page](https://3dtextures.me/about/) states that all textures are **CC0** and may be redistributed in a game. `materials/city_pavement.tres` applies this image to the Kenney pavement meshes using Godot's world-space triplanar mapping because the imported GLBs have constant atlas UVs.
- The road albedo in `vocab-box/assets/roads/asphalt_001_color.png` is **Asphalt 001** by **Katsukagi**, supplied by the project owner from [3DTextures.me](https://3dtextures.me/2017/04/05/asphalt-001/). The source calls it a seamless 1024×1024 texture, and the site's [license page](https://3dtextures.me/about/) states that its textures are **CC0** and may be included in a distributed game. `materials/road_surface.tres` uses it on the asphalt faces of the Kenney road meshes while preserving their atlas-based markings and edges. The earlier generated road image was removed.
- Remaining runtime geometry in the supermarket (non-player characters, fruit, fixtures, shelves, and room) is original procedural geometry authored for this project in `scripts/art.gd` and `supermarket.gd`. The city no longer creates visible geometry at runtime.
- Lesson writing, UI styling and synthesized ambience/effects were authored for this project. Ambient melodies and effects are deterministic Python synthesis in `tools/generate_audio.py`.
- `vocab-box/assets/sky/fair_weather_clouds.png` is an original image generated with OpenAI's built-in image generation tool for this project on 17 October 2026. It is a 2:1 additive cloud-cover texture used by the shared Godot `ProceduralSkyMaterial`; no third-party cloud asset or external model is bundled.
- `materials/painted_curb.gdshader` and `materials/painted_curb.tres` are original project materials. They apply red and warm-white curb paint directly to the existing Kenney high pavement mesh; the rest of the mesh uses the credited Stone Brick Pavement 003 image. The previous SVG and separate paint sprites were removed.
- Temporary English speech is generated locally with the installed eSpeak NG library and its `en-gb` voice. The engine is GPL-3.0-or-later; the generator links it only during development. The runtime bundle contains generated waveform output, not the library or voice databases. The GPL covers the engine; normal synthesized text output is not engine source. No cloned voice or recorded performer is used. Local package notices: `/usr/share/doc/libespeak-ng1/copyright` and `/usr/share/doc/espeak-ng-data/copyright`.
- Godot default UI font and engine resources use Godot's bundled third-party notices. The desktop runtime includes Godot's MIT license and third-party copyright notices retrieved with `Engine.get_license_text()`, `Engine.get_copyright_info()` and `Engine.get_license_info()` (saved in the bundle).
- `vocab-box/icon.svg` is the pre-existing Godot project icon; the portable bundle does not use a custom app icon.
